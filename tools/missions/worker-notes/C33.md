## Causes
1. **No `upgrade` event in ours (the mission blocker).** Orig: `decompiled/scripts/com/dchoc/dollars/world/items/states/StateOnRentVisitor.as:35` `mItemObject.registerEvent(MISSION_EVENT_UPGRADE)` -> `ItemObject.as:2787-2802` (keys: nameType "Houses", subtypes, sku). Ours: `apps/client/src/ui/social/visit.ts:211-226` (`upgrade()`) sends `add_upgrade_item` and gives the reward, but never calls `game.poll('upgrade', it.sku)`. The mapping exists (`apps/client/src/ui/missions/system.ts` `game.on("poll")` with `itemEventParameters`, `apps/client/src/game/missions.ts:817-822`), only the call is missing. Confirmed: ours' cmds have no `update_pollmanager add upgrade`.
2. **add_upgrade_item type 0 vs 1.** Ours hard-codes `type: 0` at `visit.ts:219`. Orig `StateOnRentVisitor.as:43` sends `UpgradesManager.getType()`, which is SUPER (1) when the visited neighbour is a partner: `decompiled/scripts/com/dchoc/dollars/model/upgrades/UpgradesManager.as:276-293` (`setNeighborId`, `isPartner()` -> `UPGRADE_TYPE_SUPER`), `:295` (`getType`). Every NPC is a partner (see C35.md, `FriendObject.as:121`), so orig is always type 1 on Sheik.
3. **Reward per upgrade 150 coins / 15 exp (orig, type 1) vs 100 / 10 (ours).** Orig `UpgradesManager.as:95-98` (`getDCCoinsPerUpgrade` by `mType`). Ours: `apps/client/src/ui/social/visit-logic.ts:2` (`UPGRADE_REWARD` constant, no type).
4. **Different houses upgraded for the same clicks (not determined).** Same grid, but ours upgraded 4 houses and orig 9; the clicks at y 230..410 hit an "EXPANSION FOR SALE" sign in ours and a house in orig. So the NPC city view or its rendered items differ between the two clients (cause not traced; the layout data `apps/server-rs/data/sheikLayout.json` is the same source).
5. **Visit popups.** Orig needs two clicks on the first-visit "Friend Upgrade" popup. In ours the first click closes it and the visitor toolbar's Home arrow is at about (370,455): the second click at (380,430) ends the visit (probe `ours-C33-probe/03-03-after-click2.png`, own city). The flow branches on the client (`o.p ? 1 : 2`). Cause in ours not traced.
6. **visitCity timing.** Ours registers visitCity at visit start (`system.ts:239-241`) and shows the completion popup inside the visit; orig gives it at Home. Worked around with the 39 seed (see above).

## Bugs seen
- Ours: visitor upgrades never register the `upgrade` mission event (cause 1). Blocks C32, C33, C34 (and C21/C22 are not affected: they use collectUpgraded).
- Ours: `add_upgrade_item` always `type: 0` and reward 100/10 even for partner (super) upgrades (causes 2, 3).
- Ours: visitCity completion popup appears during the visit (cause 6).

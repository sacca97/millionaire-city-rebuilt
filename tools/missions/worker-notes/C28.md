4. **Mission 67 completion chain.** Original: the `update_missions` for 67 and the storage reward `decorations_tree_18`; ours none (67 stays Up). Consequence of 1.
5. **update_missions order** (`#14/#15`): original 64, 66; ours 66, 68. Mission-list order after the claim, same family as C27 item 5.
6. **Save diffs:** only the investment-row ids (seed time), `dailyBonusInfo/dailyRewardsLastGivenDate` (timestamp) and the Missions/PollManager/storage lines from 1 and 4. companyValue: original 720000, ours 972000 (same class-wide pattern as C27 item 6).

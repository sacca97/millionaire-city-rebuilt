# C02 beat — missions 56 ("The Sheik challenge", unlockLevel 25, condition 2 = Sheik 900M) and 183 ("More CV than arab", unlockLevel 31, alt_missions)

Status: 56 EQUAL, 183 EQUAL.

## Mechanism (verified in both clients)
- The beat event fires when the profile company value reaches the NPC value (Profile.eventCheck, Profile.as:746-760; MissionDefinition.as:397-400).
- The company value is NOT a free seed: the client recomputes it from the city items on load (`apps/client/src/game/game.ts:345`
  `this.companyValue = this.companyValueBreakdown().total`). The original shows the same: attempt 1 with `companyValue=900000000` displayed
  `$872,000` and mission 56 never fired.
- So the city itself must reach >= 900M. Shortcut used for both runs: one `houses_064_002` (Taipei 101, `companyValue="999999999"`,
  itemDefinitions.xml) seeded on a free plot (-5,-12) in the own world, mode 1. The city then reads about 1.0 billion on both clients.

## Note on the chain
Mission 98 is `earn companyValue >= 1M` and unlocks the chain 308-314 (`unlockSku`); mission 122 is `earn DCCoins >= 1M`. Both
clients complete the whole chain after boot; the time to finish it differs between runs by several seconds, so the dump must come
after ~25 s (attempt b). Without that wait the `completed` dump is taken mid-chain.
